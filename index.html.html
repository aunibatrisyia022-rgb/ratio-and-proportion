01 • DISCOVER

What is a ratio?

A ratio compares two quantities.

RED : BLUE
3 : 2

3 red balls for every 2 blue balls.

Ratio tells us the relationship.

In 3 : 2, 3 belongs to the first quantity and 2 belongs to the second quantity.

TIP: Keep the order of quantities in the question.
THINK ABOUT IT

There are 3 red balls and 2 blue balls. What is the ratio of red balls to blue balls?
